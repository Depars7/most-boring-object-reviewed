## Judge criteria (RULES.txt, most important first)
1. A one-line review of a boring everyday object, funny and true — off-subject loses.
2. The laugh comes from a real, recognisable detail of the object, not a joke that would fit anything.
3. True: the observation matches how the object actually behaves; the laugh arrives with recognition.
4. Inside one line and 200 characters, every word earns its place.
5. Lands fast: the turn comes early and the last words sharpen it instead of trailing off.
Plus: "The best of these make the object's own dullness the joke."

## Why #9 wins
- **Object (criterion 1):** the dustpan is maximally boring and everyday — more boring
  than a paperclip or a rubber band, which at least have fidget value. Its dullness
  IS the joke: a career spent cleaning up after someone else.
- **True detail (criteria 2–3):** everyone who has ever swept knows the last thin
  line of dust that refuses to go into the pan, no matter how many passes you make.
  The laugh arrives with recognition, not with a gag pasted on.
- **Tight (criterion 4):** 105 characters, one sentence, every word earns its place.
- **Lands fast (criterion 5):** the turn ("entire career is cleaning up after the
  broom") comes early, and the last words sharpen it ("still can't get the last
  line of dust") instead of trailing off into sentiment.
- Runner-up #12 (thumbtack) has a sharp punch, but sitting on a thumbtack is rarer
  than fighting the last line of dust; #4 (coaster) ends on sentiment ("never once
  been thanked") rather than on the object's own detail.
